About Our Club

🌍

Our Mission

To create measurable environmental impact through collaborative projects, hackathons, and community engagement while building valuable skills for your future.

Who We Are

A diverse community of students from CS, Medicine, and other majors working together to solve environmental challenges through innovation and technology.

Our Goal
