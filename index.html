About Our Club

🌍 Our Mission

To create measurable environmental impact through collaborative projects, hackathons, and community engagement while building valuable skills for your future.

👥 Who We Are

A diverse community of students from CS, Medicine, and other majors working together to solve environmental challenges through innovation and technology.

🎯 Our Goal

Enable every member to highlight achievements like "Won X Hackathon" or "Organized event with X members" on college applications and resumes.

What We Do

💻 Hackathons

Create and participate in environmental science hackathons as a group. Build innovative solutions to real-world sustainability challenges and compete for recognition.

  • Participate in national competitions
  • Organize school-wide events
  • Collaborate on environmental tech projects

🌐 Website Development

Build and maintain our club website to showcase projects, share resources, and connect with the community.

📱 Social Media

Maintain an active Instagram presence promoting sustainability, sharing tips, and raising environmental awareness.

Why You Should Join

  1. Build Your Portfolio: Gain hands-on experience with real projects regardless of your major. Create tangible work samples that showcase your skills to future employers or colleges.
  2. Resume Booster: Stand out with achievements like hackathon wins, event organization, and leadership roles. Perfect for college applications and job interviews.
  3. Flexible Commitment: Choose your level of involvement. Whether you want to lead initiatives or participate casually, there is a place for you in our community.
  4. All Majors Welcome: From Computer Science to Medicine and beyond, every major brings unique perspectives to solving environmental challenges.
  5. Leadership Opportunities: Officer positions will open in the coming weeks after district approval. Develop leadership skills and take charge of meaningful projects.
  6. Measurable Impact: Create real change in environmental sustainability while documenting your contributions for future opportunities.
